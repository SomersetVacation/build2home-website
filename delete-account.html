ACCOUNT & DATA REQUESTS

Account deletion

Local copies and cloud account records are separate.

Current private-beta process

Self-service cloud account deletion is not yet available in the beta. This page lets you contact the team to request it; sending a request does not automatically delete records. The public-release account-deletion workflow is still being completed.

Before requesting deletion

Export and check any records, photos and documents you want to keep. Backups and originals outside Build2Home are controlled by you. Removing an app from your phone does not close its cloud account.

Request cloud account deletion

Email the team from your registered account address using the subject “Build2Home account deletion”. Include your account email and say that you want the account and associated records removed. Do not send your password.

Email a deletion request ↗

We will need to verify account ownership and review associated records before processing a request. The team will confirm the outcome; this website does not perform an automatic deletion or promise a fixed completion time.

Clear local copies from the phone

In builds that include the feature, open a home, choose Device data & privacy, export your backups and follow the typed confirmation to clear that account’s local copies and reminders. This covers its local photos, documents, equipment, maintenance and sale notes on that device. It keeps cloud records, other accounts and original files outside the app.

What may remain outside the app

Exported files, recipient copies and correspondence already sent outside Build2Home are separate. Contact the team about records subject to another person’s rights or a required retention obligation.
