Privacy information

How records are handled during the Build2Home private beta.

Updated 7 November 2026

About this notice

Build2Home is the product name, previously HomeProof. This notice describes the current private-beta approach and will be updated as the service develops. Contact the Build2Home team at build2home.support@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Records associated with your account

Sign-in information, supported home details, issue records, checklists, inspection plans and rental inspection notes use Google Firebase. Records you enter can include names, property addresses, builder details, dates, observations and repair notes. Beta access is approved separately, and home access is controlled by account permissions.

Records stored on your phone

Photo copies, attached document copies, equipment/material records, maintenance and sale-preparation information are stored locally in the phone app. These are not automatically uploaded or synchronised to another phone. Original files may contain personal information or embedded image metadata. Avoid including unnecessary information about tenants or other people.

How records are used

The app uses the details you supply to organise your home record, show walkthroughs and follow-up tasks, calculate supported reference dates and prepare the exports you request. Local phone notifications are optional and depend on device permission and settings.

Sharing and exports

You choose the content and destination of exports using your device’s share/save controls. Exported ZIP and JSON files are unencrypted. Check their contents before sending, and store them safely. Buyer handover packs and private seller archives have separate selection steps. Sharing a pack does not give someone access to your account or transfer ownership of the property.

Website and service providers

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and does not contain a signup database, advertising pixels or third-party analytics scripts. GitHub may process technical visitor information to provide hosting; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Email enquiries are sent to the team’s Gmail inbox and may be retained to handle your request.

The app’s connected records use Google Firebase. Processing locations and provider infrastructure depend on the service; we do not claim that every service processes all data solely in Australia. See Firebase’s privacy information for its service-provider practices.

Access, correction and retention

You can view and edit supported records in the app. Contact the team for access or correction requests that the app does not support. Local files remain until removed, app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. We do not promise a fixed automatic deletion period for cloud records during this beta; contact the team to request their removal. Copies you export or share are controlled by you and the recipients.

Removing data

Device-data controls remove the signed-in account’s local copies and reminders. That is separate from closing its cloud account. See account deletion for the current request process. Retain backups first: local copies can be lost through uninstalling, clearing data or losing your phone.

Privacy concerns

Email build2home.support@gmail.com with a description of your concern. Do not include passwords or unnecessary private documents. We may need to verify ownership before discussing account-specific records.
