SUPPORT & FEEDBACK

How can we help?

A useful home record starts with clear next steps.

Private beta

Build2Home is being tested by invitation on iPhone. Feature availability depends on the installed build. Existing testers may still see the earlier HomeProof name until their next update.

Contact the team

For access requests, technical problems or feedback, email build2home.support@gmail.com.

Include your app version/build, phone model, what you were trying to do and what happened. A screenshot can help if it contains no private information. Never send passwords, alarm codes, API keys or tenant documents.

Photos or documents missing?

These are stored on the phone where you added them. Signing in on another phone does not bring local files across. Use a saved backup for the original account and home, and retain your original files outside the app.

A reminder didn’t arrive

Check notification permission in iPhone Settings, the saved date and the reminder status in the app. Refresh the reminder status after changing dates or your phone’s timezone. Focus modes and other system settings can delay alerts. Maintenance alerts are not enabled on Android in this beta.

Adding a finding from an inspection report

Add the original under the inspection-report document category. Enter one finding with a page or section reference, then review and save it in the issue form. Attaching a report alone does not create issue records.

Saving an export

Save the ZIP or JSON somewhere outside Build2Home. Extract ZIP files before opening the HTML report; your browser’s Print option can save a PDF. Opening the share sheet does not confirm that a file was saved or delivered.

Building or legal questions

The team can help you use the app. For inspections, building standards, tenancy requirements or legal time limits, use the official state or territory guidance and an appropriately qualified professional.

Privacy and account requests

See our privacy information and account-deletion page for the current beta process.
