BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Private-beta terms

A few clear expectations while Build2Home is being tested.

Updated 7 November 2026

Invitation-only testing

Build2Home is a developing product. Access is approved by the team, features can vary between test builds, and a beta invitation is not a promise of ongoing availability, a particular price or a launch date.

Keep originals and backups

Keep important original documents and photos outside the app. Export and check your local backups before changing phones, clearing data or uninstalling. Build2Home is not a substitute for your original contract, inspection report, official tenancy report or required records.

Record-keeping and general guidance

Records, owner-entered dates and observations do not independently establish legal compliance, defect severity, repair quality, legal deadlines or warranty transfer. Guidance varies by jurisdiction. Check official sources and obtain inspection, tenancy, financial or legal advice appropriate to your property.

Your content and other people

Only add or share content you are entitled to use. Take care with private information about tenants, tradespeople and other people. Review every selected file and note before exporting a report or buyer pack. Do not place passwords or security/access codes in a buyer pack.

Sharing and ownership

An exported handover pack is a copy of selected information. It does not transfer title to a property, change contractual obligations, guarantee warranty eligibility or transfer app account access.

Changes and consumer rights

Testing may identify faults or require changes. Report problems to the team and keep independent copies of important records. Nothing on this page is intended to exclude rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Contact and account requests

Email build2home.support@gmail.com. Our privacy information and account-deletion page explain the current beta process.
